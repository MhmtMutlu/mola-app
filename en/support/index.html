Support

Something not working, or not clear? Write to us: destek@mm-apps.com (Turkish or English). We usually answer within a few working days.

It helps if you include your device model, iOS version, Mola's version and a sentence or two about what happened. For a personal data request, also include your Settings → Device number.

Frequently asked

How do I add my child's phone to the family?

Open Mola on your own phone and tap Add a child in the Family tab. Scan the QR code with the Camera app on your child's phone. The code is valid for 10 minutes and works for one device only. Then, together, give Screen Time access on your child's phone and choose the apps the limit applies to.

Why is Screen Time access needed?

Mola applies rules through Apple's Screen Time system; it installs no profile. On the child's phone the access is approved with the Apple account of the parent in Family Sharing. Without Family Sharing the access can still be given, but it can then be turned off in Settings; the app says so plainly.

I gave a break but my child's phone did not close right away.

The command reaches the child's phone through a notification. If the phone is offline or notifications are off, the command is applied when Mola is opened. The parent's screen shows the command's state: "Delivered" or "Applied". Scheduled rules such as school, sleep and the daily limit are stored on the phone and work without internet.

Why is "time left today" approximate?

Apple's system does not give apps the total usage time; it only reports when certain thresholds are crossed. Mola splits the daily limit into eight steps and shows the time left in those steps.

Can I see how long my child spent in which app?

No. Usage data never leaves the child's phone, and we cannot see it either. Mola is not a surveillance tool; it applies the rules a family sets together.

My tile is half-painted. Is it lost?

No. If you stop a focus session early, the tile stays half-painted and the next session carries on from there.
