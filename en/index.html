Mola

Turn screen time from a fight into an agreement.

Mola is a family screen-time app where parent and child set the rules together. Nothing is forbidden, things take a break: what is closed, why and until when is always visible.

Coming soon to the App Store

An agreement, not surveillance

Rules made together

A daily limit, school and sleep modes. The child sees every rule on their own screen; nothing is hidden.

“15 more minutes?”

A child asks for time with a short reason, a parent answers with one tap. A conversation instead of a standoff.

Focus and paint your tile

Every focus session paints a tile: outline first, then colour, finally the glaze. A half-painted tile is never lost; you finish it later.

Pomodoro on your wrist

Pick a program on Apple Watch and feel rounds and breaks change on your wrist.

Our privacy promise

  • We do not ask for a name, e-mail, phone number or location. There is no account.
  • Usage data never leaves the phone; neither we nor a parent can see it.
  • We do not read messages. Mola has neither the permission nor any way to do so.
  • Everything between your family's devices is end-to-end encrypted; our server cannot read it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
