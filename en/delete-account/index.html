Deleting your data

Mola has no account with a name, e-mail or password. If you set up a family, our server holds only random identifiers, encrypted envelopes that live at most 25 hours and, if you allowed it, a notification token. Here is how to delete them:

If you are a parent

  1. Open Mola and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Leave the family and delete my data and confirm.

Deleted immediately: your account on the server, the envelopes you sent and those addressed to you, your device record, your notification token and, if yours is the last device to leave, the family record. The encryption keys and family information on your device are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

For a child's device

So that a child cannot step out of the agreement alone, this option is not offered on a child's device. To delete a child device's record on the server, write to us as the parent:

  • Address: destek@mm-apps.com, subject: "Personal data request"
  • Include the Settings → Device number shown on the child's phone.

We delete it within 30 days at the latest and let you know. If the app is removed from the child's phone and does not connect for 180 days, the record is deleted automatically anyway.

If you use Mola only for yourself

There is no record of you on our server. Your focus sessions and tile wall are on your device only; deleting the app is enough.

Does anything remain?

E-mails you wrote to us are kept for at most 24 months after the correspondence is closed (Privacy Policy, Section 3.4). You can ask us to delete those as well.
