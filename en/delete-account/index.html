Deleting your data

Mola has no account with a name, e-mail or password. If you set up a family, our server holds only random identifiers, encrypted envelopes that live at most 25 hours and, if you turned on notifications on the parent's phone, that phone's notification token. All of it is deleted from inside the app:

If you are a parent, your own data

  1. Open Mola and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Leave the family and delete my data and confirm.

Deleted immediately: your account on the server, the envelopes you sent and those addressed to you, your device record and your notification token. If you are the only parent in the family, the records of the children's devices are deleted at the same moment. The encryption keys and family information on your phone are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

If you are a parent, your child's device

  1. In the Family tab, tap your child's name.
  2. At the very bottom, tap Remove from family and confirm.

That device's account, record and envelopes on the server are deleted immediately. Your child's phone learns this the next time it connects; it deletes the family information and the rules itself.

If you are on a child's device

You cannot leave the family alone, but you can ask to: Settings → I want to leave the family. Your request goes to your parent; when they approve, your phone's record is deleted and the rules are lifted.

If you use Mola only for yourself

There is no record of you on our server. Your focus sessions and tile wall are on your phone only; deleting the app is enough.

If you cannot reach the app

Write to us: destek@mm-apps.com, subject "Personal data request". Include the device's Settings → Device number. We delete it within 30 days at the latest. In addition, the record of a device that does not connect at all for 180 days is deleted automatically.

Does anything remain undeleted?

E-mails you wrote to us are kept for at most 24 months after the correspondence is closed (Privacy Policy, Section 3.4). You can ask for those to be deleted as well.
