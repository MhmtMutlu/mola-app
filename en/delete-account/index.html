Deleting your data

Mola has no sign-up: no username, email address or password. If you've set up a family, our server holds only random identifiers, encrypted envelopes kept for up to 25 hours and (if you turned on notifications on the parent's phone) that phone's notification token. You can delete all of these from within the app.

If you're a parent: your own data

  1. Open Mola and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Leave family and delete data and confirm.

Deleted immediately: the anonymous record created on the server for the family feature, the envelopes you sent and those sent to you, your device record and your notification token. If you're the only parent in the family, the records of the children's devices are deleted at the same time. The encryption keys and family information on your phone are deleted too. This can't be undone.

If you're a parent: your child's device

  1. In the Family tab, tap your child's name.
  2. At the bottom, tap Remove from family and confirm.

That device's anonymous record and envelopes on the server are deleted immediately. The next time your child's phone connects, it removes the family information and rules on its own.

If you're on a child's device

You can't leave the family on your own, but you can ask to: Settings → I want to leave the family. Your request goes to your parent; once your parent approves, your phone's record is deleted and the rules are lifted.

If you use Mola only for yourself

There's no record of you on our server. Your focus sessions and tile wall are only on your phone; deleting the app is enough.

If you can't access the app

Send an email to destek@mm-apps.com with "Personal data request" as the subject line. Also include the number shown under Settings → Device number. We'll delete your data within 30 days. Also, the record of any device that hasn't connected for 180 days is deleted automatically.

Is there anything that isn't deleted?

Emails you send us are kept for up to 24 months after the conversation ends (Privacy Policy, Section 3.4). You can ask for these to be deleted as well.
