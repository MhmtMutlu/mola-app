Deleting your data

Mola has no account with a name, email address or password. If you've set up a family, our server holds only random identifiers, encrypted envelopes kept for at most 25 hours and (if you turned on notifications on the parent's phone) that phone's notification token. You can delete all of these from within the app.

If you're a parent: your own data

  1. Open Mola and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Leave family and delete data and confirm.

Deleted immediately: your account on the server, the envelopes you sent and those sent to you, your device record and your notification token. If you're the only parent in the family, the records of the children's devices are deleted at the same time. The encryption keys and family information on your phone are deleted too. This can't be undone.

If you're a parent: your child's device

  1. In the Family tab, tap your child's name.
  2. At the bottom, tap Remove from family and confirm.

That device's account, record and envelopes on the server are deleted immediately. Your child's phone learns of this the next time it connects and deletes the family information and rules itself.

If you're on a child's device

You can't leave the family on your own, but you can ask to: Settings → I want to leave the family. Your request goes to your parent; once your parent approves, your phone's record is deleted and the rules are lifted.

If you use Mola only for yourself

There's no record of you on our server. Your focus sessions and tile wall are only on your phone; deleting the app is enough.

If you can't access the app

Send an email to destek@mm-apps.com with "Personal data request" as the subject line. Also include the number shown under Settings → Device number. We delete your data within 30 days at the latest. In addition, the record of a device that doesn't connect at all for 180 days is deleted automatically.

Is there anything that isn't deleted?

Emails you send us are kept for at most 24 months after the correspondence is closed (Privacy Policy, Section 3.4). You can ask for these to be deleted as well.
