Mola Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Version: 1.0

This is a translation provided for convenience. The binding text is the Turkish version: https://mola.mm-apps.com/gizlilik/ (Section 14). It serves as the notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location or advertising identifier. There is no account to create and no password.
  • Your screen-time data never leaves your phone. We cannot see which app you used for how long, and neither can a parent. Mola uses Apple's Screen Time system, which keeps usage data on the device.
  • We do not read your messages, photos or contacts. Mola has neither the permission nor any way to do so.
  • Everything between your family's devices is end-to-end encrypted. Rules, time requests, the short reason you write and the name shown inside the family are encrypted so that only your family's devices can open them. Our server cannot read them.
  • If you use Mola only for yourself (focus and the tile wall), nothing is sent to our server.
  • If you set up a family, our server in Türkiye holds only: randomly generated identifiers, encrypted envelopes that live at most 25 hours and, if you allowed notifications, the notification token Apple generated for your device.
  • We do not track you, we do not sell your data and we show no ads. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool.
  • Questions? Write to us in Turkish or English: destek@mm-apps.com

Data controller: Mehmet Mutlu (an individual developer living in Türkiye; "Mola", "we").

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Mola app for iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, the app's family server ("relay") and your correspondence with us. The App Store itself, Apple's Screen Time and Family Sharing features and Apple's notification service are subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

3.2 What passes through our family server (only if you set up a family)

A parent's and a child's phone are on different Apple accounts, so commands and requests need a meeting point. That server carries sealed envelopes only.

DataA randomly generated account number and session keys; when the account was opened and last signed in; a random family identifier and a random member identifier; a hash of the family's access token (not the token itself); when the device last connected; encrypted envelopes (sender and recipient member identifiers, an expiry time and encrypted content the server cannot read); a receipt record for envelopes addressed to everyone
PurposeDelivering a command, rule, request or answer from one family device to the family's other devices; making sure only devices that know the family key can join
Legal basisEntering into and performing a contract: KVKK Art. 5/2(c) · GDPR Art. 6(1)(b). Legitimate interest for the limits that prevent abuse: KVKK Art. 5/2(f) · GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)
RetentionAn envelope for a single device is deleted immediately when that device takes it. Other envelopes live at most 25 hours. The device record stays until you leave the family; it is deleted automatically if the device does not connect for 180 days. Accounts that never joined a family are deleted after 2 days.

The server cannot read inside an envelope: the content is encrypted on your device with your family's key (ChaCha20-Poly1305) and signed with the sending device's key (Ed25519). The server holds no name, e-mail, usage data, app list or rule.

IP address. Your device's IP address reaches the server by the nature of the connection. We do not store it with your account: our web server keeps no access log, and the IP address and device information fields of session records are left empty. To limit abuse, the address is used only in memory, in per-minute counters.

The notification contains no name, family identifier or message content. Because the server cannot read the content, the notification only says "There's an update from your family"; your device shows the text in its own language.

5. Is your data transferred abroad?

  • Data on the family server stays in Türkiye. Our server is in Türkiye. We keep no backups of this data: the content is encrypted envelopes that live at most 25 hours.
  • Apple, notifications (only if you allow them): When an envelope arrives from your family, our server sends your device's notification token and a fixed notification to Apple's servers outside Türkiye (Apple Push Notification service). This happens regularly for as long as you keep notifications on. There is no standard contract or undertaking notified to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board for this transfer; to our knowledge the Board has issued no adequacy decision for the countries involved either. KVKK Art. 9/6(a) accepts explicit consent as a basis only for occasional transfers; because this transfer is regular, it is unclear whether that basis applies and we do not claim to rely on it. We reduced the transfer to the minimum: only the token Apple itself generated and a notification without content are sent. Possible risks: this information may be processed in a country that does not offer the same level of protection as Türkiye; access by public authorities there may follow different rules. If you do not want this, simply do not allow notifications, or turn them off; Mola keeps working.
  • E-mail: E-mails you write to us are stored on Güzel Hosting's servers in Türkiye. Our replies are relayed through MailChannels Corporation (Canada; servers in North America) and are not stored there. We regard this as an occasional transfer necessary for our reply to reach you (KVKK Art. 9/6(b)).
  • Users in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: Data on the family server is processed in Türkiye. There is no EU adequacy decision for Türkiye. The transfer is necessary to provide the service you ask for (delivery between your family's devices): GDPR Art. 49(1)(b). What is transferred is random identifiers and encrypted envelopes the server cannot read.

7. Leaving the family and deletion

  • Parent: Settings → Leave the family and delete my data. Your account on the server, the envelopes you sent and those addressed to you, your device record and your notification token are deleted immediately; if yours is the last device to leave, the family record is deleted too. The keys and family information on your device are deleted as well.
  • Child's device: So that a child cannot step out of the agreement alone, this option is not offered on a child's device. To delete a child device's record on the server, write to us with its Settings → Device number (Section 9); the record of a device that does not connect for 180 days is deleted automatically anyway.
  • If you use Mola only for yourself, there is no record of you on our server. Deleting the app is enough.

Step by step: https://mola.mm-apps.com/en/delete-account/

8. Automated decisions and profiling

We make no decision about you that is based solely on automated processing and has legal or similarly significant effects, and we do no profiling. The only automated rules on the server are counters that limit abuse (for example, a device can send at most 60 envelopes an hour); when a limit is reached, only that request is refused.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it is; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties to whom it is transferred at home or abroad; request correction if it is incomplete or wrong; request deletion or destruction under KVKK Art. 7; request that these operations be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred; object to a result against you arising from analysis solely by automated systems; and claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

Under GDPR Art. 15–22 (if you are in the EEA; under the equivalent rules if you are in the United Kingdom or Switzerland) you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdrawal of consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Inside the app: for deletion see Section 7; to withdraw notification consent use iOS Settings → Notifications → Mola.

By writing to us: write to destek@mm-apps.com with the subject "Personal data request" and include your Settings → Device number. Our server does not know your name, so we cannot find your record by name or identity number; the device number lets us find it. The only readable information about you on the server is the random identifiers, times and notification token of Sections 3.2 and 3.3; we cannot open the encrypted envelopes either. If we cannot link a record to you, we will explain that (GDPR Art. 11). We do not ask for more information than needed to verify your identity. We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

Right to complain: In Türkiye you may apply to the Personal Data Protection Board (https://www.kvkk.gov.tr); in the EEA, to the data protection authority of your country.

10. Children

Mola is a family app designed to run on children's devices; for that reason it works with especially little data.

  • A child's device joins the family only through a parent: the parent shows a QR code on their own phone and the child's phone scans it. On the child's device, Screen Time access is approved with the Apple account of the parent in Family Sharing.
  • We do not ask a child for a name, e-mail, date of birth or any other contact information. The name typed during setup is visible only on the family's devices and does not have to be a real name.
  • A child's usage data, messages and app list never leave the device; neither we nor the parent can see them.
  • The only things held on the server for a child's device are the random identifiers of Sections 3.2 and 3.3 and, if notifications were allowed, the notification token. Children are shown no ads, are not tracked and are not profiled.
  • For parents: you can ask us to delete your child's device record or for any information concerning it (Section 9).

11. How we protect your data

  • Communication inside the family is end-to-end encrypted and every message is signed with the sender's key; commands such as a lock, a rule or an approval are valid only with a parent's signature.
  • The connection to the server is encrypted with TLS. Apps cannot access the server's tables directly; every operation goes through controlled functions on the server.
  • Keys stay in the device's keychain, on that device only.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs, we will notify the authorities the law requires and, where necessary, you. By the nature of the data on the server (random identifiers and unreadable envelopes), a breach would not expose your family's content.
