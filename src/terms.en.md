## 5. What Mola does and does not do

- Mola offers daily limits, school and sleep modes, time requests, a break given by the parent and focus programs.

  long the child spent in which app.
- Mola uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) system. That system belongs to Apple and Apple may change it.
  Therefore:
  - A rule may be delayed, take effect early or late, or fail to work because of an iOS update or a system fault. "Time
    left today" is an approximate value.
  - A break given by the parent or a new rule does **not reach the child's phone instantly:** it is applied when Mola
    is opened on the child's phone or when iOS gives the app a turn in the background (we do not use notifications on
    children's phones). The app shows whether the command has been applied on the device. Scheduled rules such as
    school, sleep and the daily limit are stored on the phone and are not affected by this.
  - If Screen Time access on the child's device was not approved with the Apple account of the parent in Family
    Sharing, the access can be turned off in Settings and the app can be deleted. The app says so plainly.
- For these reasons, **do not rely on Mola as the only measure ensuring your child's safety.**
