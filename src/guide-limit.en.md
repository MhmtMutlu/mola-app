# How to set a daily time limit for an app

To set a daily time limit for an app on iPhone, go to **Settings > Screen Time > App Limits > Add Limit**: choose the app and set the time. When the time is up, the app closes until the next day.

## Step by step with Screen Time

1. Open **Settings > Screen Time > App Limits** and tap **Add Limit**.
2. Choose a category (Social, for example) or individual apps inside a category.
3. Set the daily time. With **Customise Days** you can give weekends a different amount.
4. To turn on **Block at End of Limit**, you first need to set a Screen Time passcode.

iOS counts the time, and with **Share Across Devices** on it is counted together on the devices that use the same Apple Account.

## With Mola

1. Create a new daily limit in the **Today** tab.
2. Choose the apps and set the daily time.
3. Choose the strictness level.

When the time is up, those apps close until the next day; your other apps stay open. iOS still does the counting: Mola doesn't see which app you use or for how long, it is only told when the time is up.

## A time limit or a schedule?

- **A daily limit** means "I'll use this app, but this much a day". It suits social media and games.
- **A schedule** means "don't open at all during these hours". It suits work, study and sleep hours.

The two can be used together: a daily limit for the day and a sleep plan for the night.

## Common questions

**Does the limit reset at midnight?** Yes. The daily time starts again every day.

**How many minutes should I choose?** There is no single right answer. Looking at your weekly average in Screen Time and choosing a little less is a start you can keep up.
