# Support

Something not working, or not clear? Write to us: **destek@mm-apps.com** (Turkish or English). We usually answer
within a few working days.

We can help faster if you include these when you write: your device model, iOS version, Mola's version and a sentence
or two briefly describing what happened. If it is a personal data request, also include your
**Settings → Device number**.

## Frequently asked

### How do I add my child's phone to the family?

Open Mola on your own phone and tap **Add a child** in the **Family** tab. Scan the QR code that appears with the
Camera app on your child's phone. The code is valid for 10 minutes and works for one device only. Then, together,
give Screen Time access on your child's phone and, together, choose the apps the limit will apply to.

### Why is Screen Time access needed?

Mola applies rules through Apple's Screen Time system; it installs no profile. On the child's phone the access is
approved with the Apple account of the parent in Family Sharing. If Family Sharing is not set up, the access can still
be given, but in that case it can be turned off in Settings; the app says so plainly.

### I gave a break but my child's phone did not close right away.

This is expected. We do not use notifications on children's phones (we take no notification token from a child's
phone); for that reason the break you gave is applied when Mola is opened on your child's phone or when iOS gives the
app a turn in the background. The parent's screen shows the command's state: "Delivered" or "Applied". Scheduled rules
such as school, sleep and the daily limit are stored on the phone; they work without delay and without internet.

### Why is "time left today" approximate?

Apple's system does not give apps the total usage time; it only reports when certain thresholds are crossed. Mola
splits the daily limit into eight steps and shows the time left in those steps.

### Can I see how long my child spent in which app?

No. Usage data never leaves the child's phone; we cannot see it either. Mola is not a surveillance tool; it is a tool
that applies the rules the family sets together.

### My tile is half-painted. Is it lost?

No. If you stop a focus session partway, your tile stays half-painted and continues to be painted from where it left
off in the next focus session.
