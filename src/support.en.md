# Support

If you're having a problem or something isn't clear, email us at **destek@mm-apps.com** (Turkish or English).
We usually reply within a few business days.

We can help faster if you include your device model, iOS version, Mola version and a sentence or two describing the
problem. If you're making a personal data request, also include the number shown under **Settings → Device number**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create a blocking plan?

In the **Today** tab, open **Blocking plans** and tap the plus button. Give the plan a name, then choose its start
and end time, days and strictness level. When the plan starts, the apps you chose close; when it ends, they open
again. You can set up separate plans for work, study and sleep hours.

### How do I create a daily limit?

In the **Today** tab, tap the plus button next to **Daily limits**. Name the limit, choose the apps, and set the daily
time and the strictness. Once the apps you chose have been used for that long in a day, they close until the next
day. iOS measures the time; it can be off by a few minutes.

### What if I need an app that's closed?

What you can do depends on the plan's strictness level. In a **Flexible** plan, you can take a 5-minute break
whenever you like. In a **Balanced** plan, you wait 20 seconds before a break. In a **Strict** plan, there are no
breaks until the plan ends.

### How do I change which apps close?

Open **Settings → Apps to close**. You pick apps, categories and websites with Apple's own picker; your selection
never leaves your phone.

### How do I create my own focus program?

In the **Focus** tab, tap **Create your own program**, then set the focus time, the number of rounds and the break
length. You can also choose one of the ready-made programs: Pomodoro (25 min), Study (45 min), Deep focus (50 min).

### How do I add my child's phone to the family?

Open Mola on your own phone and tap **Add child** in the **Family** tab. Scan the QR code that appears with the
Camera app on your child's phone. The code is valid for 10 minutes and for a single device only. Then, together with
your child, allow Screen Time access on their phone and choose the apps the limit applies to.

### Why is Screen Time access needed?

Mola applies rules using Apple's Screen Time system; it doesn't install a profile on the phone. On the child's phone,
access is approved with the parent's Apple Account through Family Sharing. If Family Sharing isn't set up, access can
still be granted, but it can then be turned off in Settings; Mola tells you when this is the case.

### I started a break, but it didn't begin right away on my child's phone. Why?

This is expected. We don't use notifications on children's phones, and we don't collect a notification token from
them. That's why a break you start is applied when Mola is opened on your child's phone or when iOS runs Mola in
the background. You can see the status of the command on your own screen: "Delivered" or "Applied". Scheduled rules
such as school, sleep and the daily limit are stored on your child's phone; they don't need an
internet connection.

### Why is "Time left today" shown as approximate?

Apple's system doesn't share total usage time with apps; it only reports when certain thresholds are passed. That's
why Mola divides the daily limit into eight steps and shows the time left in those steps.

### Can I see which apps my child uses and for how long?

No. Usage data never leaves the child's phone, and we can't see it either. Mola isn't a surveillance tool; it's a
tool that applies the rules the family sets together.

### My tile is half-painted. Is it lost?

No. If you stop a focus session partway through, your tile stays half-painted and picks up where it left off in
your next session.
